## 1. Server in brief
MCP Bridge for Adobe Premiere Pro lets MCP clients (Codex, Claude Code, Claude Desktop, …) operate local Premiere Pro projects. It offers 283 tools, 13 context resources, and 10 prompts covering project items, sequences, timeline edits, effects, export, and more. The MCP server generates ExtendScript and hands it to a CEP panel inside Premiere (UXP is experimental) through a file-based command/response exchange in a temp directory. It is for video editors automating Premiere with AI.
