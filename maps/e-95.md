## 1. Server in brief
Ekwo OS is free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) accounting software installed on the user's own Supabase project. It covers companies, ledgers, documents (invoices), tax packs per country, bank statement import, Peppol/UBL e-invoicing, and archives. It ships a CLI (`packages/cli`), country "packs", and an MCP server (`packages/mcp`) that exposes read and write accounting tools to AI agents via the Supabase backend (RPCs and selects as the signed-in user). A managed edition is offered at ekwo.ai. Users are small businesses and accountants who want AI agents to keep their books.
