## 1. Server in brief
KnowledgeGraph MCP Server gives Claude persistent memory across conversations through a local knowledge graph of entities, observations, relations, and tags. Storage is SQLite or PostgreSQL. It is for end users who want Claude to remember facts about them, their projects, and their preferences. Tools include `search_knowledge`, `create_entities`, `add_observations`, `create_relations`, `delete_entities`, `add_tags`, and others. It is installed via npx or Docker.
