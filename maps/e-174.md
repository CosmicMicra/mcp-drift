## 1. Server in brief
UniFi MCP ("Leverage agents and agentic AI workflows to manage your UniFi deployment") is a monorepo of MCP servers for Ubiquiti UniFi: Network (devices, firewall, port forwards, content filtering, …), Protect (cameras), and Access (door access control, users, credentials, visitors), plus a shared library and a relay. Users are UniFi administrators automating their deployment with AI agents. The Access app talks to a UniFi Access controller. Tools carry permission categories/actions and an auth mode (`local_only` vs `api_key_only`).
