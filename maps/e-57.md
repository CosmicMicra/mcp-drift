## 1. Server in brief
MCP Telegram (npm `@overpod/mcp-telegram`) connects AI assistants such as Claude to Telegram via MTProto (GramJS). It runs as a **userbot** under the user's personal Telegram account, not a bot. It offers 59 tools: messaging, reactions, polls, scheduled messages, stickers, media, contacts, forum topics, groups, and account management. There is also a hosted cloud version. The session string is stored in `~/.mcp-telegram/session` (or `TELEGRAM_SESSION_PATH`), with optional MTProxy settings via env vars. It is for people who want an AI to read, summarise, and act in their Telegram chats.
