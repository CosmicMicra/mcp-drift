## 1. Server in brief
NotebookLM MCP (npm `@m4ykeldev/notebooklm-mcp`) brings Google NotebookLM into terminals, IDEs, and AI assistants (Claude, Cursor, VS Code) through an unofficial interface to NotebookLM's internal RPC API. It manages notebooks, ingests sources (URLs, YouTube, Google Drive, text), runs Deep Research, and generates "studio" content (audio, video, slides, reports, flashcards, quizzes, data tables, mind maps). Authentication uses a CDP-based browser flow (`notebooklm-mcp auth`) that extracts Google cookies to `~/.notebooklm-mcp/auth.json`. When the session expires, it is restored in the background by headless refresh or a login window.
